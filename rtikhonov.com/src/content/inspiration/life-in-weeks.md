Based on life expectancy estimates, my lifespan should be around 80 years (or till 2072). That’s 29,220 days, or 4,174 weeks, or 960 months in total. And since I’m 34 now, that means I have 16,703 days, or 2,386 weeks, or 548 months, or 45 years left.

Visualizing life in weeks/months/years/decades could be a fun and powerful way to present the past, plan for the future and be more intentional with the present.

So I decided to add it to my website.
